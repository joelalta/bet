Charging & Energy Regeneration

Multiple High-Power Energy Pathways

Explore the three complementary ways energy can enter or return to the BET battery system. Scrolling through this panel changes the active energy pathway shown in the 3D scene.

1. Rooftop Charging

The rooftop charging rail connects the BET to a purpose-built wayside charging substation for high-power, high-voltage energy transfer during planned charging windows.

The overhead arrangement avoids manually handling large charging cables and provides a clear, repeatable connection point. In the viewer, the charging structure lowers into position and blue energy bands pulse through the pantograph.

Final engineering content can explain connection verification, electrical isolation, charge authorisation and the control handshake between the substation and BET.

2. Regenerative Braking

During braking, the traction motors operate as generators. Energy from the train's motion is converted back into electrical energy, conditioned by the traction power system and returned to the BET battery system.

Regeneration reduces the energy otherwise dissipated as heat and can improve overall route efficiency. The amount recovered depends on speed, train mass, route gradient, battery state of charge and the available regenerative braking capacity.

When this section becomes active, the rooftop connection disengages and the blue energy animation moves to the BET wheels to represent braking energy flowing back toward storage.

3. Plug-In Charging Guns

Plug-in charging guns provide a physical cable connection between external charging equipment and the BET. This supports charging in depots, workshops and other locations without rooftop infrastructure.

The plug-in pathway provides operational flexibility and may support longer-duration or backup charging. Exact connector quantities, charging power and charge times remain subject to engineering approval.

Entering this section stops the regenerative wheel animation and sends the charging gun into its connection beneath the BET. The flexible cable follows the handle and settles naturally after it reaches the vehicle.
